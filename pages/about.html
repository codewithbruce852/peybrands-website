Our Story

Peybrands Solar Installations was founded with one mission: to bring reliable, affordable solar power to homes, businesses, and farms across Zimbabwe.

We understand the challenges of unreliable grid power — the costs, the disruption, the frustration. That's why we've dedicated ourselves to designing and installing solar systems that actually work, day after day, season after season.

Every project we take on is treated with the same care and attention to detail, whether it's a small home backup system or a full commercial solar installation for a farm or business.
